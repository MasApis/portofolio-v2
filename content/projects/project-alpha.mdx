# Project Alpha

## Problem

Describe the problem this project was meant to solve. Keep it short — one
or two sentences that frame why the work mattered.

## Solution

Describe what you built. Highlight the trade-offs and the parts you
chose to keep simple. Avoid listing every feature; focus on the
decisions.

## Result

Describe the outcome. Numbers are great when you have them ("reduced
load time from 2.4s to 0.8s"). If you don't, describe what you learned
or what the next iteration would look like.
